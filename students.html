How to Apply

Send an email to rifat.rashid@ewubd.edu with the subject line "Research Supervision — [Your Name]" and include:

  • Your CV and academic transcript
  • A short research statement (1 page max)
  • Your programming experience and language preferences
  • Links to any previous projects or GitHub profile
  • Which research topic interests you most
✅

What I Expect

  • Strong commitment and regular weekly progress updates
  • Clean, reproducible code with proper documentation
  • Clear written and verbal communication
  • Proactive problem-solving — come with questions, not just problems
  • Willingness to read and review related literature
  • Ambition to publish or deploy meaningful work
What you'll gain: Hands-on experience with cutting-edge AI research, guidance on publishing in reputable journals and conferences, practical skills in Python/ML/DL tooling, and a strong foundation for graduate study or AI industry roles.
