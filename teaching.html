Research Supervision

I supervise undergraduate and graduate research in applied AI, data quality, anomaly detection, and industrial/healthcare analytics. I emphasize practical research skills, reproducibility, and strong technical writing so students can publish and deploy meaningful work.

Goal-Oriented Learning

I design courses around concrete, deployable outcomes — every student should be able to build and explain a working system by the end of the semester.

🔬

Reproducible Research

Strong emphasis on clean implementation, reproducible experiments, and clear documentation — essential skills for both academia and industry.

✍️

Technical Writing

Students learn to communicate research clearly through papers, reports, and presentations — with the goal of publishing meaningful, impactful work.

🌍

Real-World Impact

Research projects are grounded in real-world problems — agriculture, healthcare, smart cities, and assistive technology — ensuring students see the broader value of their work.
